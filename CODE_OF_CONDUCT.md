# Code of conduct

Foiled is a small project and we want it to stay a good place to work. This is a short note rather than a long policy.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open where it is safe to do so.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and design, and that decisions are recorded in issues and ADRs.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Using Foiled, its issues or its data to attack, threaten or "hack back" against anyone, including people whose traffic hits a decoy.
- Posting exploit details for an unfixed Foiled containment bug in public. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact a maintainer through the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jusso-dev). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads or block people who do not follow this note.
